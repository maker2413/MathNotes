#+TITLE: Algebra

Algebra is math that uses symbols to represent numbers and solve equations.

* Origins
  The ideas and even the word "Algebra" comes from the ancient text:
  "[[https://www.loc.gov/item/2021666184/][Kitab al-Jabr wa-l-Muqabala]]" or as it translates to in English:
  "The Compendious Book on Calculation by Completion and Balancing".

  This book was written by a Persian mathematician: "Muhammad ibn Musa
  al-Khwarizmi" around 820 AD. "Algebra" is the Arabic word that translates to
  "restoration or completion".

* Abstract-ness
  We will be using the word abstract a lot throughout algebra so it is important
  to have an idea of what abstract means within the world of algebra.

  Abstraction is like taking a real thing and turning it into an idea. We can
  use a cube as an example. Even though cubes can look different in real life,
  the abstract idea of what a cube is stays the same.
